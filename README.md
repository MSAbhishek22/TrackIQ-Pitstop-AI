## 📖 The Story

Every Formula 1 fan has screamed it at a screen: *"Box! Box! His tyres are gone — why are they keeping him out?!"* Two laps later the undercut lands, the race is gone, and the strategists on the pit wall — with all their supercomputers — still got it wrong under pressure.

TrackIQ — Pitstop AI was created in November 2025 out of that exact frustration: what if pitstop strategy, the most data-rich snap decision in sport, had an AI second brain whose only job is the stop call? This repo marks the concept's home. A companion dashboard concept exists as a separate repo on this profile.

> ⚠️ **Honest status:** this repository is currently **empty** — no code, model or data has been pushed to it yet. Everything below describes the concept and the intent, clearly labelled as such. No lap-time claims, no fake demos.

---

## 🎯 The Problem

- 🛞 **Tyre life is a cliff, not a slope** — performance falls off suddenly, and one lap too late costs seconds that never come back.
- 🧮 **The stop decision is multi-variable chaos** — tyre wear, track position, traffic after the stop, safety-car probability, a rival's pace — weighed in seconds, mid-race, by humans under maximum pressure.
- 📺 **Fans see the mistake instantly** but the pit wall made it with incomplete synthesis — the data existed; the joined-up picture didn't arrive in time.

## 👥 Who It's For

- 🏁 **Race strategy enthusiasts & sim racers** — who already argue about stint lengths like it's a profession.
- 📊 **Motorsport data hobbyists** — the F1 telemetry community that lives on timing screens.
- 📺 **Broadcasters & fan apps (future)** — a "what should they do *right now*?" call is the most engaging graphic in sport.

## ✨ What It Does

> 🧪 **Concept — not yet built in this repo.** The intended system:

- 📈 **Reads the race as it unfolds** — stint age, tyre compound, pace delta and gap evolution.
- ⏱️ **Calls the window** — recommends when to box, forecasting the cost of stopping now vs. one, two, three laps later.
- ⚔️ **Simulates the fight** — undercut/overcut outcomes against the cars actually around you, not an empty track.
- 🖥️ **Shows its working** — the dashboard companion concept exists so the call is a picture, not a black box.

## 🔑 USP

**Not another results dashboard — a decision engine for the one decision that swings races.** Timing screens tell you what already happened. TrackIQ's whole reason to exist is the forward-looking question the pit wall gets paid millions to answer, made visible to anyone watching.
